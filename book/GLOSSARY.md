# Glossary and Index

**adenosine** -- Molecule that accumulates during wakefulness, creating sleep pressure; caffeine blocks its receptors
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep)*

**Akkermansia** -- A beneficial gut bacterium (Akkermansia muciniphila) associated with metabolic health and intestinal barrier integrity
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut)*

**amyloid** -- A protein that forms plaques in the brain associated with Alzheimer's disease
  *Chapters 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain)*

**ApoB** -- Apolipoprotein B; protein found on LDL and other atherogenic particles, used as a cardiovascular risk marker
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**aromatase** -- Enzyme that converts testosterone into estrogen; its activity increases with body fat and age
  *Chapters 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**autophagy** -- The cell's self-cleaning process that recycles damaged components; activated by fasting and exercise
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 12 (Chapter 12: Heat, Breath, and Light); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**BDNF** -- Brain-derived neurotrophic factor; a protein that supports neuron growth, survival, and plasticity
  *Chapters 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine); 3 (Chapter 3: Strength and Power); 4 (Chapter 4: Movement for the Long Haul); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain)*

**berberine** -- A plant alkaloid with glucose-lowering and anti-inflammatory effects, sometimes called 'nature's metformin'
  *Chapters 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism)*

**BFR** -- Blood flow restriction training; using tourniquets or wraps to restrict venous return during low-load exercise
  *Chapters 3 (Chapter 3: Strength and Power)*

**blood-brain barrier** -- A selective membrane of tightly joined cells that protects the brain from toxins and pathogens in the bloodstream
  *Chapters 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine); 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**butyrate** -- A short-chain fatty acid produced by gut bacteria that fuels colon cells and supports barrier integrity
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut)*

**CAC score** -- Coronary artery calcium score; quantifies calcified plaque in heart arteries
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First)*

**CBT-I** -- Cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia; structured behavioral program that is the first-line treatment for chronic insomnia
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep)*

**CGM** -- Continuous glucose monitor; wearable sensor that tracks blood sugar in real time
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism)*

**clusterin** -- An exercise-induced protein that reduces neuroinflammation in the brain
  *Chapters 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain)*

**CoQ10** -- Coenzyme Q10; an antioxidant critical for mitochondrial energy production, depleted by statin drugs
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**coronary artery calcium (CAC)** -- CT scan measuring calcified plaque in coronary arteries; a screening tool for heart disease risk
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First)*

**creatine monohydrate** -- The most studied and validated form of creatine supplementation; standard dose is five grams per day
  *Chapters 3 (Chapter 3: Strength and Power); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**DEXA** -- Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry; imaging method that measures body composition and bone density
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism)*

**DHA** -- Docosahexaenoic acid; an omega-3 fatty acid critical for brain structure and function
  *Chapters 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**DHEA** -- Dehydroepiandrosterone; a precursor hormone that declines with age, convertible to testosterone or estrogen
  *Chapters 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**DHT** -- Dihydrotestosterone; a potent androgen responsible for androgenetic hair loss
  *Chapters 3 (Chapter 3: Strength and Power); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**DunedinPACE** -- Epigenetic clock that measures the current pace of biological aging from a blood sample
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First)*

**eccentric training** -- Training that emphasizes the muscle-lengthening phase of a movement, producing high mechanical tension
  *Chapters 3 (Chapter 3: Strength and Power)*

**endocrine disruptors** -- Chemicals that interfere with the body's hormone systems, even at very low concentrations
  *Chapters 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals)*

**endothelial function** -- The health and responsiveness of the endothelium, the single-cell lining of blood vessels
  *Chapters 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine); 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest)*

**EPA** -- Eicosapentaenoic acid; an omega-3 fatty acid with anti-inflammatory properties
  *Chapters 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals)*

**epigenetic clock** -- Biological age test based on DNA methylation patterns that change with aging
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep)*

**fast-twitch fibers** -- Type II muscle fibers responsible for explosive, high-force movements; decline faster with age
  *Chapters 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine)*

**fasting insulin** -- Blood insulin level measured after an overnight fast; an early marker of metabolic dysfunction
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 4 (Chapter 4: Movement for the Long Haul); 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism)*

**fasting-mimicking diet** -- A low-calorie protocol designed to trigger fasting-like cellular responses while still providing some food
  *Chapters 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest)*

**finasteride** -- A drug that blocks conversion of testosterone to DHT, slowing androgenetic hair loss
  *Chapters 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**fisetin** -- A plant flavonoid found in strawberries with senolytic (senescent-cell-clearing) properties
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**FMD** -- Fasting-mimicking diet; a periodic low-calorie protocol intended to activate cellular repair pathways
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest)*

**FMT** -- Fecal microbiota transplant; transferring stool from a healthy donor to restore gut microbial balance
  *Chapters 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut)*

**free T3** -- The unbound, active form of the thyroid hormone triiodothyronine
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**free T4** -- The unbound form of thyroxine, the thyroid prohormone converted to active T3
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**GHK-Cu** -- A copper peptide that stimulates collagen synthesis and wound healing in skin
  *Chapters 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**GLP-1** -- Glucagon-like peptide-1; a gut hormone that regulates appetite and blood sugar, targeted by drugs like semaglutide
  *Chapters 4 (Chapter 4: Movement for the Long Haul); 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 15 (Chapter 15: AI as a Health Tool)*

**glymphatic system** -- The brain's waste-clearance system, most active during deep sleep, that flushes metabolic debris including amyloid
  *Chapters 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain)*

**glyphosate** -- The most widely used herbicide worldwide (active ingredient in Roundup), classified as a probable carcinogen
  *Chapters 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals)*

**Hashimoto's thyroiditis** -- An autoimmune condition in which the immune system attacks the thyroid gland
  *Chapters 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**HbA1c** -- Glycated hemoglobin; reflects average blood glucose over the previous two to three months
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism); 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**HBOT** -- Hyperbaric oxygen therapy; breathing pure oxygen in a pressurized chamber
  *Chapters 12 (Chapter 12: Heat, Breath, and Light)*

**HIIT** -- High-intensity interval training; short bursts of near-maximal effort alternating with recovery
  *Chapters 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine)*

**hippocampal neurogenesis** -- The growth of new neurons in the hippocampus, the brain's memory-formation region
  *Chapters 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain)*

**HOMA-IR** -- Homeostatic Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance; calculated from fasting insulin and glucose
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism)*

**HRV** -- Heart rate variability; the variation in time between heartbeats, used as a recovery and stress marker
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 4 (Chapter 4: Movement for the Long Haul); 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep); 12 (Chapter 12: Heat, Breath, and Light)*

**hsCRP** -- High-sensitivity C-reactive protein; blood marker of systemic inflammation
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First)*

**L-theanine** -- Amino acid found primarily in tea leaves; promotes calm alertness without sedation
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep)*

**lactate threshold** -- Exercise intensity at which blood lactate accumulates faster than it can be cleared
  *Chapters 3 (Chapter 3: Strength and Power); 15 (Chapter 15: AI as a Health Tool)*

**leucine threshold** -- Minimum amount of the amino acid leucine needed in a meal to trigger muscle protein synthesis
  *Chapters 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats)*

**Lp(a)** -- Lipoprotein(a); genetically determined cardiovascular risk factor not modifiable by lifestyle
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**magnesium glycinate** -- Magnesium bound to the amino acid glycine; well-absorbed and commonly used for sleep and relaxation
  *Chapters 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**magnesium L-threonate** -- A magnesium form shown to cross the blood-brain barrier; studied for cognitive benefit
  *Chapters 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**melanopsin** -- Light-sensitive pigment in retinal ganglion cells that sets the circadian clock
  *Chapters 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep)*

**metformin** -- A diabetes drug being studied for potential longevity benefits via AMPK activation
  *Chapters 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism); 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**microplastics** -- Plastic fragments smaller than five millimeters that accumulate in food, water, and human tissue
  *Chapters 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut); 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals)*

**migrating motor complex** -- A cyclical pattern of gut contractions between meals that sweeps bacteria and debris from the small intestine
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut)*

**mitochondrial biogenesis** -- The process of creating new mitochondria, the energy-producing organelles inside cells
  *Chapters 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine); 12 (Chapter 12: Heat, Breath, and Light); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**mTOR** -- Mechanistic target of rapamycin; a master growth-signaling pathway in cells
  *Chapters 3 (Chapter 3: Strength and Power); 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**NAC** -- N-acetylcysteine; a precursor to glutathione, the body's primary intracellular antioxidant
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**NAD** -- Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide; a coenzyme essential for cellular energy production that declines with age
  *Chapters 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**neuroplasticity** -- The brain's ability to reorganize neural connections in response to learning, experience, or injury
  *Chapters 3 (Chapter 3: Strength and Power); 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain)*

**NMN** -- Nicotinamide mononucleotide; a precursor to NAD+ studied for its potential anti-aging effects
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**NR** -- Nicotinamide riboside; another NAD+ precursor available as a supplement
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**NSDR** -- Non-sleep deep rest; a guided relaxation protocol (such as yoga nidra) that promotes recovery without actual sleep
  *Chapters 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep)*

**omega-3 index** -- Measure of EPA and DHA as a percentage of red blood cell membrane fatty acids
  *Chapters 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**osteocalcin** -- A bone-derived hormone that crosses the blood-brain barrier and supports memory and cognition
  *Chapters 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain)*

**paraquat** -- A highly toxic herbicide linked to Parkinson's disease through epidemiological and mechanistic evidence
  *Chapters 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals)*

**periodization** -- Systematic variation of training volume and intensity over time to optimize adaptation and recovery
  *Chapters 4 (Chapter 4: Movement for the Long Haul)*

**PFAS** -- Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances; persistent synthetic chemicals found in water, cookware, and packaging
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**photobiomodulation** -- Therapeutic use of red and near-infrared light to stimulate cellular energy production and repair
  *Chapters 12 (Chapter 12: Heat, Breath, and Light); 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**PM2.5** -- Particulate matter smaller than 2.5 micrometers; fine particles that penetrate deep into the lungs and enter the bloodstream
  *Chapters 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals)*

**polyphenols** -- A large family of plant compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties
  *Chapters 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut); 12 (Chapter 12: Heat, Breath, and Light); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**PRP** -- Platelet-rich plasma; a concentration of growth factors from a patient's own blood, injected for tissue repair
  *Chapters 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**quercetin** -- A plant flavonoid with anti-inflammatory properties; studied as a senolytic in combination with dasatinib
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**rapamycin** -- An mTOR inhibitor originally developed as an immunosuppressant, now studied for anti-aging effects
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**REHIT** -- Reduced-exertion high-intensity training; ultrashort sprint protocol claiming cardiovascular benefit in minimal time
  *Chapters 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine)*

**resistant starch** -- Starch that resists digestion in the small intestine and feeds beneficial gut bacteria in the colon
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut)*

**retinoids** -- Derivatives of vitamin A used topically to accelerate skin cell turnover and stimulate collagen production
  *Chapters 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**rifaximin** -- A non-absorbable antibiotic used to treat small intestinal bacterial overgrowth
  *Chapters 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut)*

**sarcopenia** -- Age-related progressive loss of skeletal muscle mass, strength, and function
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism)*

**saw palmetto** -- An herbal extract with mild DHT-blocking activity, used as a natural adjunct for hair preservation
  *Chapters 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**seed oils** -- Industrial vegetable oils (soybean, corn, canola, sunflower) high in omega-6 linoleic acid
  *Chapters 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats)*

**senescent cells** -- Cells that have stopped dividing but resist death, secreting inflammatory signals that damage surrounding tissue
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**senolytic** -- A compound that selectively destroys senescent (aged, non-dividing) cells to reduce chronic inflammation
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**SHBG** -- Sex hormone-binding globulin; a protein that binds testosterone and estrogen, reducing their bioavailability
  *Chapters 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**short-chain fatty acids** -- Metabolites (butyrate, propionate, acetate) produced by gut bacteria fermenting fiber
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut)*

**SIBO** -- Small intestinal bacterial overgrowth; a condition where excessive bacteria colonize the small intestine
  *Chapters 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut)*

**sleep spindles** -- Bursts of neural oscillations during stage 2 sleep, involved in memory consolidation and sensory gating
  *Chapters 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep)*

**slow-wave sleep** -- The deepest stage of non-REM sleep, critical for physical recovery and memory consolidation
  *Chapters 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep)*

**spermidine** -- A polyamine compound found in aged cheese and wheat germ that activates autophagy, the cell-cleaning process
  *Chapters 11 (Chapter 11: The Brain); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**sulforaphane** -- A compound from cruciferous vegetables (especially broccoli sprouts) that activates cellular detoxification pathways
  *Chapters 12 (Chapter 12: Heat, Breath, and Light); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**taurine** -- An amino acid involved in bile salt formation, antioxidant defense, and cellular membrane stability
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**time-restricted eating** -- Limiting daily food intake to a defined window, typically eight to twelve hours
  *Chapters 5 (Chapter 5: Protein, Carbs, Fats); 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest)*

**TMG** -- Trimethylglycine (betaine); a methyl donor that helps lower homocysteine levels
  *Chapters 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf)*

**TRIIM** -- Thymus Regeneration, Immunorestoration, and Insulin Mitigation trial; a small study exploring thymus regrowth with a hormone cocktail
  *Chapters 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones)*

**TSH** -- Thyroid-stimulating hormone; the standard screening test for thyroid function
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 10 (Chapter 10: Hormones); 15 (Chapter 15: AI as a Health Tool)*

**urolithin A** -- A gut-derived metabolite that stimulates mitophagy (recycling of damaged mitochondria)
  *Chapters 6 (Chapter 6: Timing, Fiber, and the Rest); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut); 14 (Chapter 14: The Supplement Shelf); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**vagal tone** -- The activity level of the vagus nerve, reflecting parasympathetic (rest-and-recover) nervous system function
  *Chapters 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine); 9 (Chapter 9: The Gut); 16 (Chapter 16: Skin, Hair, and the Long Game)*

**visceral fat** -- Fat stored around internal organs in the abdominal cavity; a stronger cardiometabolic risk factor than subcutaneous fat
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 7 (Chapter 7: Sleep); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism)*

**VO2max** -- Maximal oxygen uptake; the gold-standard measure of cardiorespiratory fitness
  *Chapters 1 (Chapter 1: Measure First); 2 (Chapter 2: The Aerobic Engine); 4 (Chapter 4: Movement for the Long Haul); 8 (Chapter 8: Heart and Metabolism); 12 (Chapter 12: Heat, Breath, and Light)*

**VOC** -- Volatile organic compounds; gases emitted by paints, cleaning products, furniture, and building materials
  *Chapters 13 (Chapter 13: Air, Water, and Chemicals)*

**Zone 2** -- Low-intensity aerobic training zone where fat oxidation is maximized and mitochondrial function improves
